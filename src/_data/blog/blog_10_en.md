Music Match is a card game made by Source 1 Media in Leeuwarden, with QRSong! as the technical partner behind the scanning and playback. It is worth explaining on its own terms, because it is a different game from the timeline one most people know, and it suits a different kind of evening.

## How it plays

Everybody gets a hand of cards. The aim is to be first to play all of yours.

A card comes into play, its song gets scanned, and you can put down a card from your hand if it matches on something: the artist, the genre, the decade, and so on. If you cannot match, you draw.

If you have played card games, the shape is immediately familiar. The difference is that the matching happens on music rather than on colours and numbers, so the round is decided by whether you recognise something rather than by what you were dealt.

## Why it plays differently from a timeline game

A timeline game is a solo puzzle taken in turns: you get a song, you place it, you are right or wrong. Music Match is a hand-management game with other people in it. You are holding cards, watching what goes down, and deciding when to use the one good card you have been saving.

Two practical consequences:

**It is faster.** Rounds are short and the pace does not depend on one person thinking.

**It rewards breadth over precision.** Knowing roughly what era and genre something is beats knowing exact release years. That makes it much friendlier to a mixed table, because nobody needs to be a music obsessive to be competitive.

## Who it is for

Groups where people's music knowledge is uneven. A timeline game can be brutal on somebody who does not follow music closely; matching on genre and decade is a far lower bar, and a guest who knows nothing still plays a real part.

It also works better than the timeline game for younger players, for the same reason.

## FAQ

### What is Music Match?

A music card game where you match cards from your hand to the song in play on artist, genre or decade, racing to empty your hand. It is made by Source 1 Media, with QRSong! providing the scanning and playback.

### How is it different from a music timeline game?

A timeline game asks you to place a song by its release year, one player at a time. Music Match is a hand-management game: faster rounds, and it rewards knowing roughly what something is rather than knowing exact years.

### Is it good for people who do not know much about music?

Better than a timeline game, yes. Matching on genre or decade is a much lower bar than naming a release year, so a mixed table stays competitive.

### Do I need an app?

Yes, the free QRSong! app, which plays the songs without showing the title. Scanning with a phone camera would display the song name and end the round.

### Can I make a Music Match deck from my own playlist?

No, Music Match is a fixed published game. If you want cards from your own playlist, that is what QRSong! decks are, and they play a timeline game, bingo and a quiz.
