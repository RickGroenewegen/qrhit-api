For years the cards were the only part of QRSong! you could make your own. Your photos on the front, your logo, your colours. Then everybody opened the same teal app to scan them.

That always felt like a missed step, especially at birthday parties and company events, where the app is the thing people hold in their hands all evening. So we built App Designer.

## What it does

App Designer changes the look of the free QRSong! app for your cards: the background (a colour, a gradient or your own photo), the text and accent colours, the font, your logo, the animations and even the help text that explains the game.

Your guests do not install anything new. They use the same free QRSong! app for iPhone and Android, and as soon as they scan the first card of one of your playlists, the app switches to your design. Scan a card from another set and it shows that set's look instead.

## How it works

1. Log in to [My Account](/[lang]/my-account/app-design) with the account you ordered your cards with.
2. Design the app. The designer shows the real app while you work: the home screen, the scan screen, the playing screen, settings and help.
3. Switch it on. It is a one-off [price:appDesign] for your whole account, not a subscription and not a price per playlist.
4. Scan a card. That is it.

You can design and preview everything before you pay, so you know exactly what you are getting.

## One design, or one per playlist

Your default design applies to every playlist you bought, and to the ones you buy later. If one set needs to look different (the office party deck in company colours, the birthday deck in neon), you give that playlist its own design.

There is a third option I did not expect to matter as much as it does: the standard look. If you order a set as a present for someone else, you probably do not want your party theme to follow the cards into their living room. Set that playlist to the standard QRSong! look and it stays neutral.

## Changing it later

Whenever you like. Change the colours the week before the party, swap the photo an hour before the guests arrive. The next card that is scanned shows the new version, and nobody has to update anything.

## Let AI pick the colours

If you have a photo you love but no idea which colours go with it, upload it and press the AI button. It looks at the photo and picks a text colour, an accent, a button style and a font that fit. Then you fine-tune whatever you want.

## When it is worth it, and when it is not

Honestly: for a game night with friends, the standard app is fine. App Designer earns its keep when the evening has a theme. A milestone birthday, a company party in brand colours, a pub quiz that wants to look like the pub, a Christmas party in red and gold. That is when a guest picks up a phone, sees the birthday photo behind the scan button, and smiles before the first song even plays.

If you are a business that wants the app fully branded for a large event, we still make [hand-made themes](/[lang]/business) as part of the business offer. App Designer is the self-service version for everybody else.

## FAQ

### Do my guests need a different app?

No. Everyone uses the free QRSong! app from the App Store or Google Play. It switches to your design as soon as the first card of one of your playlists is scanned.

### What does App Designer cost?

A one-off [price:appDesign] for your whole account. It covers all your playlists, including the ones you buy later.

### Can each playlist have its own design?

Yes. Your default design covers every playlist, and any single playlist can get a design of its own or the standard QRSong! look.

### Can I change the design after the cards are printed?

Yes. The design lives in the app, not on the cards, so you can change it whenever you like. The next scan shows the new version.

### Which screens of the app change?

The home screen, the scan screen, the playing screen, settings and the help screen, including your own help text for your guests.
